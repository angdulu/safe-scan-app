# Product

## Register

product

## Users
People checking if consumer products (food, cosmetics, medicine) are safe for their specific chronic conditions (e.g. 당뇨, 고혈압, 임산부) or allergies in active, fast-paced retail environments.

## Product Purpose
A real-time, privacy-first, zero-latency safety scanner that extracts ingredients from products (image or text) and maps them to user-defined health profiles to provide clear safety summaries and follow-up AI chats.

## Brand Personality
Clean, quiet, soft, and premium. Guided by Apple-style Cupertino layouts and the smooth, typography-driven minimalism of Toss UI.

## Anti-references
Websites with overly loud, saturated colors, neon primaries, and harsh high-contrast boundaries. No generic SaaS template telling styles (Inter-only, card-within-card clutter).

## Design Principles
1. Quiet Clarity: Rely on generous whitespace, soft-tinted neutrals, and premium typography rather than loud, saturated colors to draw focus.
2. Apple/Toss Smoothness: Implement soft rounded corners, light shadows, and responsive micro-interactions that make transitions feel frictionless.
3. Utility First: Simplify layouts to ensure the scan action and the safety verdict are immediately readable with zero visual noise.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Ensures readable typography with appropriate font scaling and tint ratios that meet WCAG accessibility standards without defaulting to jarring high-contrast lines. Proactive support for reduced motion.
